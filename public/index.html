Insova: medicine shortage information for Irish community pharmacies

Is it short? Can I get it? What else can I give? The same questions come up at the counter every time a medicine is short. Insova answers them in one place, so Irish community pharmacists spend less time chasing shortages.

What Insova does today

  • Collects the HPRA medicine shortage register every morning and shows what appeared, what left the register, and which expected return dates moved.
  • Keeps a dated copy of the register every day since 3 August 2026, so it can show how often a return date has been pushed back.
  • Shows alternatives taken only from the HPRA List of Interchangeable Medicines, and which products in each group are short or not marketed.
  • Searches every authorised and withdrawn medicine in Ireland.
  • Lets pharmacists star the products they dispense, so changes to them come first.

In development

Wholesaler availability, and earlier warning before a shortage reaches the counter. Neither is available yet.

Insova is information only. It never substitutes, orders or dispenses, and it is not a patient record system.
