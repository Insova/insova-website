2. What we collect, and why

Insova is a tool for community pharmacists. Most of what it shows comes from the HPRA's public registers and is not personal data at all. What follows is everything we hold about people.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal data to anyone.
  • We don't show advertising, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers on this site.
  • We don't use your data to train artificial intelligence models.
  • We don't use your data to make automated decisions about you.
  • We don't send marketing emails unless you have asked for them.

4. Patient information

Insova is not a patient record system, and it is not designed to hold patient information. Please never enter a patient's name, date of birth, address, PPS number, or anything else that could identify a patient, in any part of Insova, including notes and unlicensed medicines records.

If patient information is entered by mistake, tell us at contact@insova.ie and we will remove it.

6. Where it is held

Our database, which holds everything in the table in section 2 apart from website visits and emails, is hosted in the European Union, in Ireland.

Some of our providers, including Railway, Cloudflare and Google, are based in the United States or operate worldwide, so some data, mostly IP addresses from visiting the website, may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens it is protected by safeguards approved under EU law, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account, your list, and feedback: for as long as your account is open. If you ask us to close your account, we delete them within 30 days.
  • Unlicensed medicines records: kept while your pharmacy uses Insova, because they are shared with colleagues. Records you added are removed or anonymised when your account is closed.
  • Emails with us: up to two years, then deleted, unless an ongoing matter needs them.
  • Security logs and website request logs: kept by our providers for a short period for security purposes, then deleted.

If the law requires us to keep something for longer, for example tax records, we keep only what is required, for only as long as it is required.

8. How we protect it

  • Everything travels over encrypted connections.
  • Access to data in the database is controlled row by row: you can only reach your own list, and only your own pharmacy's records.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • Only the people who run Insova can access the database, and only to run and support the service.

If a breach ever put your data at risk, we would tell the Data Protection Commission and, where the law requires, tell you directly.

9. Your rights

Under data protection law you have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, and close your account;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • take it with you in a common, machine-readable format.

To use any of these, email contact@insova.ie. We will reply within one month, and there is no charge. We may ask you to confirm who you are before acting on a request.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, Ireland's data protection regulator, at dataprotection.ie. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

10. Cookies and browser storage

Insova uses no advertising or analytics cookies. Two things are stored in your browser, and both are needed for the service to work:

  • Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.
  • A single offline page, shown if you open the installed app with no internet connection. Insova deliberately stores no shortage data in your browser, so it can never show you an out-of-date register as if it were current.

Our security provider, Cloudflare, may set a short-lived cookie to tell people apart from automated attacks. It is not used to track you.

11. Information from the HPRA

The medicine shortage register, the List of Interchangeable Medicines, and the lists of authorised and withdrawn medicines shown in Insova are published by the Health Products Regulatory Authority. They describe medicines, not people.

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this notice and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell account holders by email before it takes effect.
