Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

Written to be read rather than to protect us. If anything here is unclear or looks wrong, tell us and we will fix it.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Who is responsible

Insova is an early-stage project run by Jack Kennedy and Isobel Hynes in Cork, Ireland. We are not yet incorporated as a company, which means the two of us are the data controllers personally rather than a limited company being responsible. When that changes we will update this page and say so.

Contact us about anything on this page at contact@insova.ie. A real person reads it.

The short version

We hold as little as we can get away with.

If you have only visited the website, we hold no account details about you at all. If you have an Insova account, we hold your name, your work email, which pharmacy you belong to, and the notes and records you create in the app. We do not sell anything to anybody, we do not run advertising, and there are no tracking or analytics cookies on this site.

We do not want and do not ask for patient information. Please do not put any into Insova.

What we collect

If you just visit the website

Our web host and our network provider record technical information automatically, as every website does: your IP address, browser type, which pages loaded and when. This is used to serve the site and to block automated attacks, and nothing else. We do not have analytics software on this site and we do not build a profile of visitors.

If you have an Insova account

WhatWhy we have it
Your name and work email To create your account, sign you in, and know who we are talking to.
Your password Stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. Neither we nor they can read it.
The pharmacy you belong to, and your role To show you the right data and keep other pharmacies' records away from you.
Sign-in times, IP address and browser Recorded by our authentication provider so that we can spot unauthorised access to an account. We do not use it to monitor how much you work.
Your watchlist and any notes on it So the app can show you the medicines you are tracking. Your watchlist is private to you: colleagues at your pharmacy cannot see it and neither can we in the normal course of running the service.
Unlicensed medicine records you create To build a shared record of what was supplied during a shortage and whether it was reimbursed. Colleagues at your pharmacy can read yours; only you can edit it.
Please do not enter patient information.

The notes fields in Insova are for supply information, not clinical records: which product, which supplier, what the wholesaler said. Do not type patient names, initials, dates of birth, prescription numbers or anything else that identifies a person. Insova is not a patient record system, it is not built to hold that kind of information, and it should not be used as if it were.

If you have already entered something like that, email us and we will remove it.

What we do not collect

  • No patient data of any kind.
  • No prescription or dispensing records. If that ever changes, it will be through a separate written agreement with your pharmacy, and this page will be updated first.
  • No payment or card details. We do not currently charge for Insova. If we start, payments will be handled by a specialist provider and card details will never touch our systems.
  • No tracking cookies, no advertising, no analytics scripts, no session recording.

Why we are allowed to hold it

Under the GDPR, our legal bases are:

  • Contract. We need your account details to give you the service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests. Keeping the service secure and working, which covers server logs, sign-in records and blocking automated attacks. We think this is what any user would expect and it involves the minimum data needed.

We do not rely on consent for anything, because we do not do anything optional with your data that would need it.

We use a small number of specialist providers to run the service. They process data on our instructions only, under written agreements, and none of them are permitted to use it for their own purposes.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or give it to anyone else unless we are legally required to.

Cookies and what is stored in your browser

There are no analytics or advertising cookies on this site, so there is no cookie banner to click through.

When you sign in, your browser stores a session token so you stay signed in between pages. That is strictly necessary for the service to work and it does not track you anywhere else. Signing out removes it, as does clearing your browser storage.

Cloudflare may set a security cookie to distinguish real visitors from automated traffic. That is also strictly necessary and is not used for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Account details: while your account is open, and 30 days after you ask us to close it, so that an accidental deletion can be undone.
  • Your watchlist and notes: deleted with your account.
  • Unlicensed medicine records: these belong to your pharmacy rather than to you personally, so they remain with the pharmacy if you leave. If you would rather yours were deleted, ask and we will delete them.
  • Server and security logs: kept by our providers for short retention periods, typically days to weeks.
  • Email you send us: kept while it is useful to keep, then deleted.

The Irish medicine shortage data in Insova is public information published by the Health Products Regulatory Authority. It contains no personal data and we keep it indefinitely, because a dated archive of it is the point of the product.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of everything we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your account and your data
  • Restrict or object to what we do with it
  • Send your data to you or someone else in a portable format

Email contact@insova.ie. We will respond within 30 days and there is no charge. We are a two-person operation, so in practice it is usually much quicker than that.

If you are not happy with how we have handled something, you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie. We would rather you told us first so we can fix it, but that route is yours regardless.

Security

Everything is served over an encrypted connection. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are not readable by us. Access to each pharmacy's data is enforced in the database itself rather than only in the app, so a fault in the interface cannot expose another pharmacy's records.

We are a small team building something early. If you find a security problem, please tell us at contact@insova.ie rather than posting it publicly, and we will fix it and tell you when we have.

What Insova is not

Insova is an information tool, not a clinical one.

It never substitutes, orders or dispenses a medicine, and nothing in it is clinical advice. Alternatives are shown only where the HPRA List of Interchangeable Medicines lists them, cited by their IC code. Every decision remains the pharmacist's, in consultation with the prescriber.

Changes to this page

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this page and change the date at the top. For anything significant, we will email account holders rather than expecting you to notice.
